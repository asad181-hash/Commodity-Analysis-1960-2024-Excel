# Commodity Price Analysis (1960–2024) — Excel

## Project Overview
An Excel-based data analysis project exploring historical commodity price trends from 1960 to 2024 to identify long-term movements, volatility, and potential business insights.

## Objectives
- Analyze historical price trends across multiple commodities.
- Compare commodity performance over time.
- Identify periods of significant price changes and volatility.
- Develop insights that support data-driven decisions.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Excel formulas and statistical analysis
- PivotTables, charts, and dashboard visualizations

## Commodities Analyzed
Coffee, Copper, Corn, Sugar, Soybean, Soybean Oil, Wheat, Cotton, Oats and Lumber.

## Methodology
1. Inspect and prepare the dataset.
2. Clean and structure the data.
3. Analyze price trends and changes over time.
4. Compare commodity performance and volatility.
5. Present findings using Excel charts and visualizations.

## Key Findings
1. Soybean being the most expensive Commodity.
2. Average Price decreased from $5.2 to $4 in last 2 years.
3. Sugar being the most profitable commodity over the years. 13.8% annual growth.
4. Also Sugar being the most volatile commodity. High Risk == High Profit.
5. Corn being the stable commodity with relatively stable pricing.
6. Copper & Wheat having the most growth years. 36 overall.
7. Consecutive growth years by Oats - 6 years and consecutive decline years by Cotton 5 years.
